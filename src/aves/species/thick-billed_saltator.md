A grosbeaklike bird found in the canopy of montane forests; often accompanies mixed-species flocks. Note the broad white eyebrow and black mustache, which in males contrasts with a dark gray head and back and a buff throat and belly. Females have an olive-green back, a gray face, and a buff throat and belly. Females resemble Green-winged Saltator, but with a buff (not white) throat and a heavier bill.
